> HOW TO PLAY

Every digit and the +/− sign is built from glowing segments, like matchsticks. Click a lit segment to pull it into your buffer, then click any empty slot to wire it back in. Segments can travel between digits and the sign: take the vertical bar off a + to make a −, or drop one into a 9 to get an 8.

Use exactly the number of moves shown (or fewer) to make the equation true. The = sign is hard-wired. Every digit must stay a real digit, and multi-digit numbers can't start with 0.

Many puzzles have more than one fix. Find them all for bonus credits. Click a segment you're holding (or its empty origin) to put it back for free.
